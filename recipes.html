Banoffee Chia Pudding
Teacher check required: confirm the 2026 practical sequence, current labels, individual plans and local allergy controls before preparation.

Ingredients (serves 2\)

Chia

  1. 1 tbsp chia seeds
  2. ½ cup (250ml) coconut milk
  3. ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  4. 1 tbl maple syrup

Caramel

  1. ½ cup dates, soft

(pre-soaked for 10mins is warm water)

  1. 30ml coconut milk
  2. ½ tsp sea salt

Caramelised banana

  1. ½ banana
  2. 1 tsp honey

Topping options

  1. ½ tsp cinnamon
  2. 1 tsp shredded coconut
  3. 1 tsp cacao nibs

Method

  1. For the chia, in a large bowl mix the chia seeds, 250ml coconut milk and vanilla. Set aside in the fridge to allow the chia to absorb the coconut milk and swell.
  2. For the caramel sauce, in a food processor, add dates, 60ml coconut milk and salt. Blitz until smooth and a sauce is created.
  3. For the topping, heat a medium frying pan over a medium to high heat. Add rice malt syrup and sliced banana. Cook until golden on both sides.
  4. To serve, using a serving glass, add caramel sauce to the bottom, top with chia and finish off with caramelised banana and your choice of topping.
Dairy Free Chocolate Coconut Custard
Teacher check required: confirm the 2026 practical sequence, current labels, individual plans and local allergy controls before preparation.
  1. demonstrates hygienic handling of food to ensure a safe and appealing product FT5-1

• demonstrate appropriate selection of techniques and equipment used in food preparation

  1. investigate circumstances which lead to specific food needs, for example: ![Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander histories and cultures icon][image1] ![Ethical understanding icon][image2] ![Literacy icon][image3] ![Difference and diversity icon][image4]
  2. Aboriginal cultural considerations, e.g. not consuming or preparing foods associated with totems
  3. health status or disability, e.g. diet-related disorders
  4. religious beliefs
  5. lifestyle choices, e.g. vegetarianism

Ingredients (Serves 2\)

  1. 200ml (1/2 can) coconut milk
  2. 2 egg yolks
  3. 2 tbs (30g) caster sugar
  4. 2 tsp (10g) cocoa powder
  5. 2 tbs (20g) cornflour
  6. 1 tsp vanilla extract
  7. Pinch of sea salt

Method:

  1. In a large saucepan, over a medium heat warm the coconut milk until you start to get a light steam off the top. Do not allow to boil.
  2. In a bowl whisk together the egg yolks, sugar, cocoa and cornflour.
  3. Slowly add one third of the warmed milk mixture to the egg mixture while continuously whisking (this will temper the egg yolks and help prevent them from curdling).
  4. Add the tempered egg mixture back into the saucepan and heat over medium-low heat while whisking continuously for approximately 5 minutes.
  5. The custard is ready once it starts to thicken and coats the back of a spoon.
  6. Remove from heat and whisk in the vanilla.
  7. Strain the custard through a sieve and pour into a container, place plastic wrap on the top of the custard, put the lid on and set in the fridge.
Gluten Free Pineapple Upside Down Cake
Teacher check required: confirm the 2026 practical sequence, current labels, individual plans and local allergy controls before preparation.

› identifies, assesses and manages the risks of injury and WHS issues associated with the handling of food FT5-2

• investigate circumstances which lead to specific food needs, for example:

– Aboriginal cultural considerations, e.g. not consuming or preparing foods associated with totems

– health status or disability, e.g. diet-related disorders

– religious beliefs

– lifestyle choices, e.g. vegetarianism

• demonstrate safe and hygienic work practices, for example: (ACTDEK045, ACTDEP050)

  1. personal hygiene
  2. food safety, e.g. the food danger zone, cross-contamination
  3. safe work practices
  4. use of PPE

Ingredients (serves 2\)

Topping

  1. 1/4 cup (50g) brown sugar
  2. ¼ cup (50g) butter, melted
  3. 6 pineapple rings, drained

(keep 2 tbs of juice/syrup for cake)

  1. 3 glacé cherries, halved

Cake

  1. 1/2 cup (100g) raw caster sugar
  2. 100g butter
  3. 1 tsp vanilla extract
  4. 2 eggs
  5. 2 tbs pineapple juice/syrup from can
  6. 3/4 cup (100g) gluten free self-raising flour
  7. 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180C.
  2. Grease a 20cm cake tin.
  3. For the topping, in a large bowl, cream the softened butter and brown sugar together until creamy. Spread over the base and a quarter of the way up the sides of a 20cm round cake tin.
  4. Arrange 6 pineapple rings on top (reserving the syrup for later), then place 6 glacé cherry halves in the centre of each pineapple ring. Set aside.
  5. For the cake, in a large bowl cream the butter and sugar with an electric mixer. Add the vanilla, eggs and pineapple juice. Mix until well combined.
  6. Spoon the cake mixture on top of the pineapple and spread it evenly.
  7. Bake for 30 \- 35 mins.
  8. Leave the cake to rest for 5 mins, then turn out onto a plate.

High Protein Flank Steak Quinoa
Teacher check required: confirm the 2026 practical sequence, current labels, individual plans and local allergy controls before preparation.
  1. discuss the impact of food allergies and intolerances on an individual’s food choices
  2. demonstrate appropriate selection of techniques and equipment used in food preparation
  3. demonstrate safe and hygienic work practices, for example: (ACTDEK045, ACTDEP050) ![Work and enterprise icon][image1]
  4. personal hygiene
  5. food safety, e.g. the food danger zone, cross-contamination
  6. safe work practices
  7. use of PPE

Ingredients (serves 2\)

Steak

  1. 2 tsp olive oil
  2. Pinch sea salt
  3. 2 x Flank steaks (minute steak)

Quinoa

  1. ½ cup quinoa, rinsed
  2. 1 cup (250ml) water
  3. 1 cup baby spinach
  4. 1 tbs olive oil
  5. ¼ tsp sea salt

Chimichurri sauce

  1. ½ cup flat leaf parsley
  2. 1 clove garlic, minced
  3. 1 tsp dried oregano leaves
  4. 2 ½ tbs light olive oil
  5. 1 tbs red wine vinegar
  6. ½ tsp lemon juice
  7. ⅛ tsp ground cumin
  8. ¼ tsp sea salt
  9. ⅛ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. For the quinoa, in a medium saucepan, add the rinsed quinoa and water and bring to the boil uncovered over a medium to high heat. Decrease the heat to a gentle simmer and cook the quinoa until all the water is absorbed, keep decreasing the heat as needed. Do not stir the quinoa.
  2. Once all of the water is absorbed, remove from heat and put the lid on. Let the quinoa steam for 5 mins before using a fork to fluff and stir through baby spinach, olive oil and salt.
  3. For the steak, heat the oil in a large frying pan. Season both sides of the steak with sea salt. Once the pan is hot, add the steak and cook on high for 1 minute each side. Remove from pan and set aside to rest before slicing.
  4. For the sauce, finely chop parsley, garlic and oregano. (Alternatively pulse in a food processor)
  5. In a small bowl, add the fresh chopped sauce ingredients, then mix in olive oil, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, cumin, salt and pepper. Mix well until combined. Set aside in a small bowl.
  6. To serve, add quinoa to a plate, top with sliced beef and finish off with chimichurri sauce.

Tip: Rinsing quinoa removes its natural coating, called saponin, which can make it taste bitter or soapy.

Keto Ricotta Pancakes
Teacher check required: confirm the 2026 practical sequence, current labels, individual plans and local allergy controls before preparation.
  1. investigate the role of nutritionally modified foods in meeting the requirements of individuals with specific needs, for example: ![Difference and diversity icon][image1]
  2. low kilojoule
  3. low fat, salt and/or sugar
  4. high fibre
  5. gluten free

Ingredients (Serves 2\)

  1. 4 large eggs
  2. 1 cup ricotta cheese
  3. 1/4 cup (60ml) full cream milk
  4. 1 tbs vanilla extract
  5. ½ tbs lemon zest
  6. 2 tbs raw sugar
  7. 6 tbs (42 grams) coconut flour
  8. ½ tsp baking soda
  9. 2 tbs butter for cooking

Topping options

Option 1

  1. 3 strawberries, sliced

Option 2

  1. 1 rasher bacon, cooked and sliced

Option 3

  1. 1 tbs butter
  2. 2 tbs lemon juice

Method

  1. In a large bowl whisk the eggs. Then add ricotta, milk and vanilla. Whisk until nice and smooth. (Alternatively add to a food processer and blitz until smooth).
  2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Add some butter to grease the pan.
  3. Pour approximately 2 tablespoons worth of batter into the pan pancake.
  4. Cook the pancakes until golden brown and puffed, 3-4 minutes per side. Grease the pan with the remainder of the butter and repeat with the second batch of batter.
  5. Prepare your choice of topping and serve over pancakes.
Paleo Peri-Peri Chicken Sweet Potato Fries
Teacher check required: confirm the 2026 practical sequence, current labels, individual plans and local allergy controls before preparation.

\> applies appropriate methods of food processing, preparation and storage FT5-5

  1. describe nutritional requirements for each stage of the life cycle ![Critical and creative thinking icon][image1] ![Literacy icon][image2]
  2. demonstrate appropriate selection of techniques and equipment used in food preparation.

• demonstrate safe and hygienic work practices, for example: (ACTDEK045, ACTDEP050)

– personal hygiene

– food safety, e.g. the food danger zone, cross-contamination

– safe work practices

– use of PPE

Ingredients (Serves 2\)

Peri Peri Marinade

  1. 1 large chicken breast
  2. 1 tbs olive oil
  3. 1 tsp chilli flakes
  4. 1 tsp minced garlic
  5. 2 tbs lemon juice
  6. 1 tsp smoked paprika
  7. 1 tsp oregano
  8. 1 tsp brown sugar
  9. ¼ tsp sea salt
  10. Black pepper to taste

Sweet Potato Fries

  1. 1 large sweet potato,

peeled and cut into chips

  1. 3 tbs vegetable oil
  2. ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  3. ¼ tsp chilli powder
  4. ¼ tsp garlic powder
  5. ⅛ tsp white pepper
  6. ½ tsp sea salt

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220C.
  2. Line a tray with baking paper.
  3. In a large bowl mix together the oil, smoked paprika, chilli powder, garlic powder, white pepper and salt. Add the sweet potato chips and toss until evenly coated.
  4. Spread the potato chips in a single layer onto the prepared baking sheet, being careful not to overcrowd them. Bake for 15-25 minutes turning once, or until golden brown.
  5. In a medium bowl, mix together the marinade ingredients. Set aside.
  6. Place your chicken breast on a chopping board and butterfly the breast by placing your hand flat on top of it. Carefully use a sharp knife to slice into one side of the breast, starting at the thicker end and ending at the thin point. Be careful not to cut all the way through to the other side. Open the chicken breast so it resembles a butterfly.
  7. Add the chicken breast to the marinade mixture and toss well to coat the chicken evenly.
  8. Place the chicken on a lined baking tray and place in oven to cook for 15 – 20 minutes. Chicken is cooked if juices run clear or by checking the internal temperature reaches 75°C with a thermometer.
  9. Remove chicken from the oven and rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  10. Serve with sweet potato chips.

Vegan Carrot Cupcakes
Teacher check required: confirm the 2026 practical sequence, current labels, individual plans and local allergy controls before preparation.
  1. demonstrate appropriate selection of techniques and equipment used in food preparation
  2. demonstrate safe and hygienic work practices, for example: (ACTDEK045, ACTDEP050) ![Work and enterprise icon][image1]
  3. personal hygiene
  4. food safety, e.g. the food danger zone, cross-contamination
  5. safe work practices
  6. use of PPE

Ingredients (Makes 12\)

  1. 2 cups (250g) plain flour
  2. 1 ½ cups (300g) brown sugar
  3. 1 tsp baking powder
  4. 1 tsp baking soda
  5. ½ tsp sea salt
  6. 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  7. 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  8. 2 1/3 cups (250g) grated carrot

\- approx. 2-3 carrots

  1. 2 flax eggs (2 tbs ground flaxseed

meal with 6 tbs hot water)

  1. ½ cup (125ml) vegetable oil
  2. 1 tsp vanilla extract
  3. 1 tbs apple cider vinegar

Lemon Buttercream Frosting

  1. 3 cups (375g) icing sugar
  2. 1/3 cup (75g) nuttelex margarine

or similar

  1. ½ tsp vanilla extract
  2. 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  3. 2 tbs (30ml) lemon juice

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180C.
  2. Line a muffin pan with cupcake liners.
  3. In a small bowl, make flax eggs by mixing the flax meal with hot water. Set aside for 5 minutes to thicken.
  4. In a large bowl sift the flour. Add the brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, sea salt, ground cinnamon, ground nutmeg, and grated carrot. Mix until combined.
  5. Add the flax eggs to the bowl along with the oil, vanilla and apple cider vinegar. Mix until it forms a thick batter.
  6. Scoop the cake mixture evenly across the 12 cupcake liners.
  7. Bake in the oven for 20 \- 30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  8. Let it rest in the muffin pan for 1 minute, then transfer to a wire rack to cool before frosting.

Frosting

  1. In a large bowl add sugar, margarine, vanilla, apple cider vinegar and lemon juice. Mix on a low speed with an electric mixer until smooth.
  2. The frosting should be thick enough to spread, if not add a little more icing sugar until you reach the desired consistency.
  3. Evenly spread or pipe the frosting onto the cupcakes.
