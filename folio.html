Stage 1 of 8

The need and design brief

Action: Define the problem, intended user and required result without recording unnecessary private medical details.

Evidence: A clear brief showing the person, occasion, verified need, constraints and items marked Teacher to confirm.

Think: What must this food response achieve, and what information is authoritative?

Sentence starter

The design brief asks me to ... for ... The verified requirement is ...

Saved on this device.

Stage 2 of 8

Success criteria

Action: Turn the brief into a small set of observable tests.

Evidence: Four to six criteria covering the verified need, nutrition, practicality, cost, safety and respectful practice.

Think: How will you know whether the finished response meets the brief?

Sentence starter

The result will be successful if ... This can be checked by ...

Saved on this device.

Stage 3 of 8

Research and ideas

Action: Use reliable evidence and generate at least two genuinely different responses.

Evidence: Source notes plus two labelled ideas with strengths, limits and questions.

Think: What evidence changes your design decision?

Sentence starter

Source ... supports ... One possible response is ... Another is ...

Saved on this device.

Stage 4 of 8

Chosen idea and plan

Action: Select one direction and make it practical.

Evidence: A justified choice plus ingredients, equipment, sequence, timing and cost notes.

Think: Why is this the strongest option against your criteria and constraints?

Sentence starter

I selected ... because it meets ... The main trade-off is ...

Saved on this device.

Stage 5 of 8

Safer production readiness

Action: Identify the controls and teacher-owned stop points before practical work.

Evidence: Current label checks, hygiene and cross-contact controls, plus explicit questions for the teacher.

Think: What must be verified before any ingredient is opened?

Sentence starter

Before production I will verify ... I will stop and ask the teacher if ...

Saved on this device.

Stage 6 of 8

Making evidence

Action: Record one authorised checkpoint that proves a decision, process, problem or change.

Evidence: A dated observation or teacher-authorised photo with a meaningful caption; not a competence claim.

Think: What happened, what did you change, and what does the evidence prove?

Sentence starter

During production ... I changed ... because ... This evidence shows ...

Saved on this device.

Stage 7 of 8

Testing against criteria

Action: Test the food response using the criteria written in Stage 2.

Evidence: Results for each criterion, supported by observations or a teacher-authorised final photo.

Think: Which criteria were met, partly met or not met, and what is your evidence?

Sentence starter

Criterion ... was met/partly met/not met because ...

Saved on this device.

Stage 8 of 8

Evaluation and improvement

Action: Make a justified final judgement and name one realistic next change.

Evidence: A conclusion tied to the brief, criteria, evidence and limitations.

Think: How well did the result solve the problem, and what would you change next time?

Sentence starter

Overall, the response ... The strongest evidence is ... Next time I would ... because ...

Saved on this device.
